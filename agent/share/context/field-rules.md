Field rules:
- likeness: true ONLY for new use of the person's face, voice, name or image beyond the original asset (endorsement, spokesperson ad, merchandise with their image, AI/synthetic replication). Reposting the original clip or photo is NOT likeness.
- commercial: true if a brand, sponsor or business uses it for marketing or revenue, including a business account reposting it. If unclear, true.
- aiGeneration: creating new AI content from the asset or person. aiTraining: using it as model training data.
- sublicensing: passing rights on to anyone else.
